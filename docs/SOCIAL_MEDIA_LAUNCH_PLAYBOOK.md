## 3. High-Converting Short-Form Video Scripts (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)

Mobile puzzle games thrive on **curiosity, pattern recognition, and "I can do that faster" ego triggers**. Here are 5 video blueprints:

---

### Video 1: "The Wrap-Around Paradox" (Curiosity & Mechanic Hook)
* **Goal**: Show the signature toroidal wrap-around mechanic.
* **Duration**: 18–25 seconds.
* **Visual**:
  * 0–3s: Screen recording of a piece stuck on the far-right column (col 4). The target is on the far-left (col 0).
  * 3–8s: Finger swipes the row to the **right** instead of left. The piece exits the screen on the right and wraps around to Column 0 instantly!
  * 8–15s: Solve the remaining 2 pieces in 3 quick, snappy moves. Win screen pops with 3 gold stars.
* **On-Screen Text**:
  * `0s`: "Most people try to drag it across 4 tiles..."
  * `4s`: "Watch what happens when you push it off the screen 🤯"
  * `12s`: "Toroidal geometry is crazy."
  * `16s`: "Link in bio to play Level 1-150 free"
* **Sound**: Trending calm lo-fi beat or snappy acoustic pop.
* **Caption**: "When a board has no edges, the shortest path is usually backwards 🌀 #puzzle #indiegame #mobilegaming #shiftpuzzle #brainteaser"

---

### Video 2: "Level 38 Has Only 7 Moves — 95% Fail" (Ego Challenge)

* **Duration**: 22–30 seconds.
* **Visual**:

  * Move 3 naively solves the yellow diamond but displaces the green echo.
  * Undo! Show rethinking.
  * Move 4: Column 3 shift downwards to park the piece.
  * Move 5–7: Final cascade locks all 4 gems simultaneously into their glowing targets. Par achieved!
* **On-Screen Text**:
  * `0s`: "Level 38: Par is 7 moves."
  * `3s`: "Rule: You CANNOT move individual pieces. You shift whole rows."
  * `15s`: "Found the echo chain on move 5."
  * `20s`: "Can you solve it in fewer moves?"
* **Caption**: "Can you beat Level 38 in 7 moves? Drop your high score below 👇 APK link in bio! #puzzlechallenge #iqtest #gaminglife"

## 4. Reddit & Community Promotion Strategy

### Recommended Subreddits:
* `r/puzzlevideogames` (Strictly puzzle enthusiasts, values design depth)
* `r/AndroidGaming` (Loves standalone, offline-first indie APKs)
* `r/IndieDev` & `r/SoloDevelopment` (Great for devlogs and feedback)

### Reddit Post Template (`r/puzzlevideogames` / `r/AndroidGaming`)
* **Title**:
  `I spent the past year designing a 5x5 toroidal sliding puzzle with 150 handcrafted levels (Free, Offline-first, Zero P2W)`
* **Body**:
  ```markdown
  Hey everyone!

  Like many puzzle fans here, I've grown frustrated with modern mobile puzzle games bloated with pay-to-win boosters, energy bars, and compulsory video interruptions after every 2 moves.

  I wanted to make something tactical, clean, and deeply mathematical. Today I released **Shift Puzzle v1.0.0** as a standalone Android release.

  ### The Core Mechanic:
  * Instead of moving single pieces, you shift entire rows and columns across a 5x5 matrix.
  * The edges wrap around **toroidally** — sliding a tile off the right edge loops it right back to the left.
  * Moving one piece inadvertently alters the coordinates of other pieces sharing that axis, creating cascading combinatorial puzzles.

  ### Key Specs:
  * **150 Handcrafted Levels** across 15 chapters (audited with exact BFS shortest-path solvers).
  * **100% Offline-First**: Stored completely on-device in sandboxed storage.
  * **Zero Pay-to-Win**: No purchasable cheats, no move locks.

  The standalone APK is directly available on our site:
  👉 https://shift-puzzle-4cj.pages.dev/

  Would love to get feedback on the difficulty curve from Chapter 3 onward!
  ```
